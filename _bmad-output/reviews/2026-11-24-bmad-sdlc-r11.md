## Risks and trade-offs

- **Moving review to QA (D1)** is the right separation, but it turns `build.review` into a cross-role step in the middle of the build loop. The menu must offer it as a handoff, not hide it; otherwise teams will override it.
- **Persisting the role (D2)** adds ledger state that can go stale when a workstream is paused or reopened. Decide whether `role_override` clears on `resume`, `resize`, and `done`; the plan assumes it clears.
- **Treating conductor actions as role-free (D3)** removes the last handoff before close. That is the intent, but it means "QA closed this" is never true; say so in the close summary.
- **Collapsing the overlays (S1)** trades a short role-specific page for one wider table. Keep the role column next to the action id so a role-skilled reader still scans one column.
